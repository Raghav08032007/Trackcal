# Consent: TrackCal usability session

We're asking you to try a fitness and food-logging app and think aloud while you use it. The session takes about 30–40 minutes.

- **What you'll do:** six short tasks on a phone we provide, using a demo account. You won't use your own account or your own health data.
- **What we record:** the phone screen and the facilitator's notes. If you agree below, we'll also make a screen recording. Your face is not recorded.
- **How it's used:** only to improve the app's design. Results are reported without names, for example as "P3".
- **How long we keep it:** the recording is deleted within 30 days. Notes are kept without your name.
- **Your choice:** you can skip any task, stop at any time, or ask for your notes to be deleted, with no reason needed.
- **Age:** you must be 18 or older to take part in this session.
- **Contact:** if you have questions or want your data removed later, contact ____________________ (facilitator).
