## Features they have that TrackCal doesn't

| Feature | Who has it | Why it matters | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Apps in the Play Store and App Store** | All of them | Most Indian users install only from the Play Store | **Must** |
| **Health sync**: Health Connect, Apple Health, Fitbit, Garmin; steps, heart rate, sleep and weight imported automatically | MFP, Cronometer, MacroFactor, HealthifyMe, Strava | Less typing; calories burned become real | **Must** |
| **Adaptive calorie target**: works out your true daily burn from weight trend and intake, and adjusts weekly | MacroFactor (its main selling point), MFP Premium | Users stop guessing; the best retention driver in the category | **High** |
| **AI nutrition chat** ("what should I eat for dinner?") grounded in your log | HealthifyMe (Ria), MFP, Lifesum, Fitia | Everyday daily-use reason to open the app | **High** |
| **Strength progression**: suggests next weight/reps; muscle recovery map | Fitbod, Freeletics | Workouts that grow with the user | **High** |
| **Micronutrients** (iron, B12, calcium, vitamin D…) shown clearly | Cronometer (80+ nutrients) | Important for vegetarian Indian diets; our IFCT data already has many of them | **High** (cheap) |
| **GLP-1 / medication programmes**: dose log, side effects, protein focus | HealthifyMe, Noom, MFP | The biggest weight-loss trend of 2025–26 | Medium |
| **Wearable readiness** (sleep, heart rate variability) | Fitbit, Whoop, Oura; HealthifyMe with devices | Smarter training days | Medium (after health sync) |
| **Smartwatch app** (Wear OS / Apple Watch) | Strava, NTC, Fitbod | Rest timers and reps on the wrist | Later |
| **Huge global food database** with restaurant chains | MFP (millions of items), Lose It! | Users abroad and eating out | Medium: add Open Food Facts India and chain menus |
| **Human coaches and dietitians** | HealthifyMe, Noom | Paid tier and trust | Medium: coach booking exists; needs payments (E2) |
| **Psychology course** (habits, emotional eating) | Noom | Long-term behaviour change | Later |
| **Live / on-demand video classes** | cult.fit, Apple Fitness+, NTC | Follow-along sessions | Later: our 3D coach can do follow-along instead of video |
| **Lab report reading** (blood tests explained) | HealthifyMe | Health-aware plans | Later; medical review needed |
| **Continuous glucose monitor (CGM)** | HealthifyMe Pro, Levels, Lingo | Premium niche | Not now |
| **Sign in with Google/Apple; family plans** | Most | Faster sign-up | **High** (sign-in), later (family) |

## Features TrackCal has that they don't

| TrackCal feature | Nearest competitor | Why it's different |
|---|---|---|
| **Food tracking and camera workout coaching in one app**: reps counted and form corrected live, then logged with the meals | Food apps have no camera coach; camera form apps (Strive, FormCoach…) have no food tracking | No one else does both, and the calorie burn feeds the food day |
| **16 languages**, including 11 Indian languages and right-to-left Urdu and Arabic, also in the live coach's spoken cues | HealthifyMe: English plus a few; camera form apps: English only | Biggest gap in the Indian market |
| **Free barcode scanning** | MFP put barcode scanning behind Premium | A simple, visible reason to switch |
| **Indian food that "just works"**: IFCT data, cooked portions ranked first, regional dish names | HealthifyMe is strong here; MFP and Cronometer weak | We measured 100/100 on everyday Indian searches |
| **Workouts that fit your equipment**: swap or skip with a coach-style substitute | Fitbod has equipment profiles; food apps and camera apps don't | Works at home with nothing |
| **Camera runs on the phone; no video leaves the device** | Some camera apps also do this; most food apps' photo AI uploads | A trust and privacy story |
| **Budget meal planner + pantry + price comparison + shopping list** | Rare in calorie apps (meal-kit apps do parts) | Fits Indian households and students |
| **Game layer**: adventure, quests, XP, a customisable 3D character that levels up | Streaks and badges at most | Fun for younger users |
| **Community events with QR check-in, communities and challenges from your own records** | Strava has clubs and challenges; food apps have forums | Brings gyms and colleges into the app |
| **Works in any browser, offline, without installing** | All competitors need an install | Low-storage phones and quick trials |
| **Free printable health and progress reports** | Usually Premium | Easy to share with a doctor |
| **Price**: most features free; Premium planned well below HealthifyMe | MFP US$79.99/yr; HealthifyMe higher | Value in price-sensitive markets |

Shared with many (not a differentiator): AI meal photo logging, recipe import from a link, reminders, water and weight
tracking, fasting timer, GPS activities.

## What to add, in order

1. **Play Store app** (Android TWA wrapper) + **Google sign-in**
2. **Health Connect sync**: steps, workouts, weight, sleep, heart rate (Android first)
3. **Adaptive calorie target**: weekly burn estimate from weight trend and intake
4. **AI nutrition chat** in all 16 languages, grounded in the user's own log, with the medical safety rules we already use
5. **Micronutrient view and alerts**: iron, B12, calcium, vitamin D and protein for vegetarians
6. **Strength progression**: next-set suggestions and a muscle recovery map, using the camera's rep and form history
7. **GLP-1 / medication companion**: dose and side-effect log, protein and hydration targets
8. **Open Food Facts India + chain restaurant menus**
9. **3D coach follow-along classes**: our answer to video classes, in every language
10. **WhatsApp reminders and sharing** (India)
